# Customer-Churn-Analysis
Analyze customer data to identify patterns behind subscription cancellations. Evaluate user activity, engagement levels, and behavioral trends to determine key factors that lead to churn and suggest improvements for retention.

CUSTOMER CHURN ANALYSIS

OVERVIEW
This project analyzes user behavior to understand why customers cancel their subscriptions. By evaluating engagement levels, support interactions, and monthly charges, the analysis identifies high risk patterns and suggests data driven retention strategies.

OBJECTIVES
1. Identify behavioral trends that lead to customer churn.
2. Evaluate the relationship between support calls and cancellation rates.
3. Analyze engagement scores to determine a threshold for user retention.
4. Provide actionable suggestions to improve customer loyalty.

ANALYSIS WORKFLOW
Phase 1: Behavioral Data Profiling. Examining the distribution of monthly logins and subscription plans.
Phase 2: Engagement vs Churn Correlation. Using statistical correlation to find the strongest predictors of churn.
Phase 3: Visualization. Creating heatmaps and distribution plots to show the difference between active and churned users.
Phase 4: Insight Generation. Tagging users as high risk based on activity drops and high support friction.

KEY FINDINGS
1. Low Engagement Score is the primary predictor of churn.
2. Users with more than 6 support calls in a month have a significantly higher cancellation rate.
3. Monthly charges show a secondary impact on churn for users on the Basic plan.
